Zhizheng Wu.

I am an Associate Professor in the School of Data Science at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen.

My research focuses on speech generation, speech foundation models, neural audio codecs, and trustworthy audio AI. I am interested in how machines generate, understand, evaluate, and protect human voice.

I direct the M.Sc. programme in Artificial Intelligence and Robotics, serve as Deputy Director of the Shenzhen Key Laboratory of Cross-Modal Cognitive Computing, and hold courtesy and joint appointments with the School of Artificial Intelligence and Shenzhen Loop Area Institute.

Before joining CUHK-Shenzhen, I worked at Meta, JD.com, Apple, the University of Edinburgh, and Microsoft Research Asia. I received my Ph.D. from Nanyang Technological University.

Research and open source

Our group studies generative speech, speech language models, and audio safety and evaluation. Current work includes zero-shot speech and voice generation, efficient speech representations, human preference alignment, deepfake detection, and interactional privacy.

  • Amphion — an open-source toolkit for audio, music, and speech generation.
  • Emilia — a large-scale multilingual dataset for speech generation.
  • Merlin — an open-source neural-network speech synthesis toolkit.
  • ASVspoof — a benchmark initiative for speaker verification spoofing and countermeasures.
