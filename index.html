Speech, Audio, and Generative AI

Research, open-source systems, and academic leadership in speech AI.

Dr. Zhizheng Wu is currently an Associate Professor at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (CUHK-Shenzhen), a Jointly Appointed Professor at Shenzhen Loop Area Institute, Director of the Artificial Intelligence and Robotics Taught Post-Graduate Program at CUHK-Shenzhen, and Deputy Director of the Shenzhen Key Laboratory of Cross-Modal Cognitive Computing.

Professor Wu has been selected as a National-level Young Talent. He has been consecutively listed in Stanford University's "World's Top 2% Scientists" and has received multiple Best Paper Awards. Prior to his current position, he worked at Meta, Apple, JD.com, the University of Edinburgh, and Microsoft Research Asia.
