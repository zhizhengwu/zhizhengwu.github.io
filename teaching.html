Teaching approach教学理念

I encourage students to connect the ideas behind modern AI with disciplined implementation: understand the signal, question the metric, reproduce the result, and build something that can be tested in the real world.我希望学生既懂现代 AI 背后的思想，也能扎扎实实把它做出来：吃透信号，敢于质疑指标，亲手复现结果，最后做出经得起真实场景检验的东西。
